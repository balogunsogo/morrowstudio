(Overview)

A ten-week exhibition of contemporary furniture, objects and graphic design, held in a former tram depot in Glasgow. We designed the graphics, catalogue and campaign.

The curators wanted a show that someone sheltering from the rain could follow, so every decision started with the label rather than the poster.

Write labels for the visitor, not the lender.

(Catalogue)

A catalogue that works as a guide on the day.

96 pages, pocket-sized, following the route room by room. Each work has one photograph, one paragraph from the maker and the label number used on the wall.

(Labels & wayfinding)

Every label answers the same three questions in the same order: who made it, what it’s made of, why it’s here. The route is marked on the floor in each section’s colour.

Outcome: [X] visitors over ten weeks; the catalogue was reprinted in week [Y].

“The comments book was full of people saying they didn’t think design was for them, until now.”
