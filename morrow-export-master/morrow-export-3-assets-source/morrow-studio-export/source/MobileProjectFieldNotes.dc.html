(Overview)

A nonfiction publisher whose backlist was the business, but online it was one scrolling page of covers. We built a site that sells the books directly and makes older titles findable.

Readers and the trade use the same book page; booksellers’ details sit in a quiet panel at the foot.

(Catalogue)

Every book gets a real page, not a product tile.

An opening extract, the author’s note, reviews, format and price, and links to the author’s other books. Browse by subject, series or author.

Sell the reading, not just the book.

(Shop)

Checkout stays on the site, with signed copies where available and a gift subscription of four books a year shown on every book page.

Outcome: direct sales grew to [X]% of online revenue, up from [Y]%.

“Our ten-year-old titles are selling again because people can finally find them.”
