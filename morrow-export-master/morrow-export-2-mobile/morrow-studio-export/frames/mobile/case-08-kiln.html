Kiln

Wood-fired bread and coffee, branded around the oven.

Year
2024
Services
Identity
Packaging
Signage
Client
Kiln Coffee

(Overview)

A bakery and coffee bar built around a wood-fired oven in a Leeds railway arch. The owners wanted to roast and sell their own beans, so the brand had to move from counter to shelf.

The oven is the business, so it became the identity: a mark drawn from its arched mouth, a palette of embers and flour.

Fig. 01–02 — House roast, 250 g; cups in the oven’s ash glaze

The oven is the logo.

(Stamps)

Labelled each morning, by whoever’s on the bench.

Rubber stamps for the mark, roast and date replace printed labels, so the range can change weekly. Each roast has its own ink colour.

(Menus & signage)

A chalk rail with painted headings and items written daily; prices on removable strips. Outside, one illuminated mark at the colour of the fire.

Outcome: retail coffee grew to [X]% of revenue within a year.

“People take the bag home and it still feels like the bakery.”

— Head baker & co-owner, Kiln
