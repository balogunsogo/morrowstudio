Meridian

Renting a home, without the sales pitch.

Year
2023
Services
Website
Digital design
Development
Client
Meridian Estates

(Overview)

A long-term landlord of rental apartments in Manchester. The old site read like a sales brochure; renters wanted the price, the floor plan and the move-in date.

Every listing shows all three on the first screen, with no form to fill in first. Photography is of real apartments, never renders.

(Availability)

Listings sync from the property-management system every hour, so a home disappears as soon as it’s let. Viewings book straight into the lettings team’s calendar.

(Floor plans)

Plans first, photographs second.

Renters compare plans between listings, so every apartment type has a clean, consistent plan above the gallery.

Fig. 02–03 — Ancoats; Salford Quays
