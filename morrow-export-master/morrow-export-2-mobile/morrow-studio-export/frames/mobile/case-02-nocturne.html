Nocturne

Three nights of music, lit by a single source.

Year
2026
Services
Art direction
Campaign
Photography
Client
Nocturne Festival

(Overview)

A winter music festival held over three nights in buildings that are usually closed after dark. The campaign had to sell the setting before the line-up was announced.

One rule for every image: a single light source and nothing else, across posters, venue photography, the trailer and the stage lighting brief.

Fig. 02–03 — Library follow-spot; print hall stage

If it can be seen in daylight, it isn’t Nocturne.

(Campaign)

Posters that switch between day and night.

Ink on white for daytime sites, white on deep blue for late-night and underground sites. The light bar sits in the same place on both, so mixed runs still read as one campaign.

(Rollout)

Three waves: venues, then the trailer, then the line-up. Each wave reused the same photographs with more information added, so early posters never went out of date.

Outcome: [X] of 3 nights sold out before the line-up was announced.

“People bought tickets for the buildings. The line-up was a bonus.”

— Festival director, Nocturne
